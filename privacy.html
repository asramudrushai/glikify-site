Privacy notice

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Glikify is run by Sara Durmush, a sole trader based in Derby, UK. This notice explains what personal information I collect through glikify.com, why, and what your rights are. If you have any questions, email hello.glikify@gmail.com.

What I collect

  • Enquiry form: your name, email address, business name (if you give it), the service you're interested in, and your message.
  • Gilly, the website assistant: the questions you type, and your name and email address if you choose to leave them.
  • Emails you send me: whatever you include in them.

Why I use it

I use your information only to reply to your enquiry and, if we work together, to provide the service you've asked for. The legal basis is legitimate interests (replying to someone who has contacted me) and, where we agree to work together, taking steps to enter into and carry out a contract. I don't sell your information, and I don't add you to a mailing list unless you ask me to.

Where it's stored

Enquiries and chat details are stored securely using these services, which act on my behalf:

  • Render, which hosts this website and Gilly.
  • Google (Google Sheets and Gmail), where enquiries are recorded and emailed to me.

Some of these providers may store data outside the UK. Where they do, they use legal safeguards approved for UK data transfers.

This website also loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers. The site doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies.

How long I keep it

I keep enquiries for up to 12 months if we don't go on to work together, and then delete them. If we work together, I keep records for as long as needed for the work and to meet legal and accounting requirements.

AI twins and your likeness

If you choose an AI twin service, I only create your AI twin with your clear written permission. I use it only to make content for you, and I delete it, along with the setup footage, if you stop working with me or ask me to.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information I hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, object to how I use it, or ask me to stop using it. Just email hello.glikify@gmail.com and I'll reply within one month.

If you're unhappy with how I've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.
